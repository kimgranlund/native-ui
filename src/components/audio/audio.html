<n-audio>

CSS-only audio container with token-aware border-radius and responsive sizing. Wraps a native <audio> element.

Basic

<n-audio>
  <audio controls>
    <source src="track.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
  </audio>
</n-audio>

Border Radius

radius="sharp"
radius="round"
radius="pill" 
<n-audio radius="sharp">
  <audio controls>...</audio>
</n-audio>

<n-audio radius="round">
  <audio controls>...</audio>
</n-audio>

<n-audio radius="pill">
  <audio controls>...</audio>
</n-audio>

Inside a Container

Now Playing 
<n-container>
  <header>
    <span>Now Playing</span>
  </header>
  <div class="body">
    <n-audio>
      <audio controls>
        <source src="track.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
      </audio>
    </n-audio>
  </div>
</n-container>
