<n-card-header> / <n-card-body> / <n-card-footer>

Composable sub-containers for structuring surfaces. CSS-only. Work inside n-card, n-drawer, n-panel, or any flex column.

n-card-header — Basic

Card Title

Body content with the default padding and scrollable overflow.

 
<n-card>
  <n-card-header>
    <span slot="label">Card Title</span>
  </n-card-header>
  <n-card-body>
    <p>Body content here.</p>
  </n-card-body>
</n-card>

n-card-header — Leading & Trailing Slots
