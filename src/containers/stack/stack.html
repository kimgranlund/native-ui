<n-stack>

Flex layout utility. Vertical or horizontal stacking with configurable gap, alignment, and wrapping. CSS-only container.

Form layout

Remember me Sign In

  <n-input placeholder="Email address"></n-input>
  <n-input placeholder="Password" type="password"></n-input>
