API

Attributes

AttributeTypeDefaultDescription
cols"1" | "2" | "3" | "4" | "5" | "6"auto-fillFixed column count. Omit for responsive auto-fill.
min"8rem" | "10rem" | "12rem" | "14rem" | "16rem" | "20rem" | "24rem""16rem"Minimum column width for auto-fill mode.
gap"0" | "1" | "2" | "3" | "4" | "6" | "8""2"Gap multiplier (--n-space * N).
padding"none" | "tight" | "regular" | "relaxed""none"Padding scale.
align"start" | "center" | "end" | "stretch" | "baseline"stretchAlign items along the cross axis.
