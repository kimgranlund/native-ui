A2A Sessions

A support dashboard powered by two AI agents running in parallel sessions. The Conversation Agent handles the customer interaction — collecting information and suggesting solutions. The Analytics Agent watches the conversation and surfaces relevant customer data, order history, and risk scores. Both agents render to isolated surfaces on the same page, managed by a single NSessionManager.
