3. Voice & Copy

Clear. Calm. Confident. Human. -- Clinical language demands precision without anxiety.

Do / Don't

Do

"See how the differential diagnosis evolved with each new result."

"3 care gaps identified. Here's the recommended action for each."

"Lab results updated the working diagnosis from 30% to 85% confidence."
Don't

"Our revolutionary AI-powered platform leverages cutting-edge..."

"Comprehensive, robust solution that seamlessly integrates..."

"Utilizing next-generation machine learning algorithms..."

Terminology

Decision trace (not "audit log") -- our term for captured clinical reasoning

Clinical Context Graph -- our structured knowledge representation

Care gap (not "compliance gap") -- patient-centered framing

6. Data Visualization

Token-based colors. SuisseIntl throughout. Intent families encode clinical meaning.

Chart Color Ramp (Accent)

Rules

1. Use intent colors -- success for positive trends, danger for negative, accent for neutral series

2. SuisseIntl Mono for axis labels, values, and annotations

3. Token-based fills only -- no hardcoded hex values

4. Must work in both light and dark mode

5. No gradients -- flat OKLCH fills only

Rules

  • Minimum clear space: equal to the cap height of the "A" on all sides
  • The "A" contains a negative-space triangle -- never fill, crop, or obscure it
  • On light surfaces: dark fill. On dark surfaces: white fill. On accent: white fill.
  • Never stretch, rotate, recolor to off-brand colors, add effects, or place on busy backgrounds
  • Minimum display width: 120px (below this, letterforms lose legibility)
